Competitive Intelligence — What They're Doing That You're Not

We analyzed 6 direct competitors. Here's what's working for them that Echo Chamber Media should adopt:

Top Things to Steal

Steal from: IN COLOR STUDIOS
Client Logo Carousel — "As Seen Working With"
IN COLOR displays 20+ recognizable brand logos (CBS, MGM, Caesars, etc.) in a scrolling carousel. This instantly signals credibility. If Echo Chamber has worked with ANY recognizable brands, venues, or events — add a logo bar to the homepage. Even wedding venues (Bellagio, Wynn, Red Rock) count.
Steal from: IN COLOR STUDIOS
Blog / Case Study Content Strategy
IN COLOR has an "ICS Story" blog with case studies targeting long-tail keywords. This is the BIGGEST gap — Echo Chamber has ZERO blog content. Competitors who blog consistently rank for 50+ keywords that pure service pages can't capture. Start with 2 posts/month: one case study ("Behind the Scenes: Red Rock Canyon Wedding Film") and one educational piece ("How Much Does Wedding Videography Cost in Las Vegas?").
Steal from: LEVY PRODUCTION GROUP
Story-Driven About Page — "The Brothers" Narrative
Levy leads with their founder story (35 years, two brothers, 14,000 sq ft facility). This builds massive trust. Echo Chamber's About section is thin. Create a dedicated /about page with Billy's story — filmmaker background, why Las Vegas, the vision. Real stories convert better than feature lists.
Steal from: LIGHTEN FILMS
Video Testimonials Over Text Reviews
Lighten Films features video testimonials from real couples. For a VIDEO production company, having only text reviews is a missed opportunity. Shoot 5 quick 30-second testimonials from happy clients. These convert 2-3x better than text and double as portfolio content.
Steal from: THE LOU EFFECT
Wedding Platform Presence — WeddingWire, The Knot, Zola
The Lou Effect ranks on wedding aggregator platforms where couples actually search. These platforms dominate the "wedding videographer Las Vegas" SERP — not individual company websites. Claiming profiles on WeddingWire, The Knot, and Zola gives you access to thousands of active couples without fighting for organic rank.
Steal from: FIVESIX PRODUCTIONS
Nationwide Positioning + City-Specific Landing Pages
FiveSix positions as nationwide while having location-specific pages. Echo Chamber should create area-specific pages for Henderson, Summerlin, North Las Vegas, and surrounding areas. Each one captures local search volume with minimal effort.
Steal from: ALL COMPETITORS
Google Reviews Volume — You Need 20+ Reviews ASAP
Most competitors have 15-50+ Google reviews. Reviews are a MAJOR local ranking factor. Start a review generation campaign: email past clients with a direct Google review link. Target 20 reviews in the next 60 days. Each one strengthens your local SEO.
