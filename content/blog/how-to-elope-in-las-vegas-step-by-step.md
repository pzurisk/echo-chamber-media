Eloping in Las Vegas is easier than most people expect. You need a license, someone to marry you, and a plan for the day. Here is the order we suggest, from the couples we film.

## 1. Pick your date

Start with the date, because everything else hangs on it. Chapels, officiants, and filmmakers book by date. If you are planning last minute, reach out anyway. Short notice dates often still work.

## 2. Get your marriage license

In Las Vegas the license comes from the Clark County Marriage License Bureau. Check the bureau's current requirements before you travel so nothing surprises you at the counter. When you book with us, we send you a simple checklist so you know what to bring.

## 3. Choose where you will say your vows

You have three good options.

- **A chapel.** Book any chapel you like. We coordinate with them on timing and camera rules. The Luciano and Muriel film on our [elopements page](/elopements) was shot at the Little Church of the West.
- **Downtown.** Say your vows, then walk Fremont Street under the lights.
- **The desert.** Red rock at golden hour, then city lights after dark.

You do not need a chapel. Bring an officiant and your license and we will help you pick a spot. A rooftop works too.

## 4. Decide how much of the day you want on film

Most elopements are short, so you do not need all day. A one hour chapel visit is a full story. Two hours lets you add a walk downtown. Four hours gives you room for the desert and the city.

Our three packages follow that shape: [The Chapel at $500, Downtown at $750, and Desert & Lights at $1,000](/elopements#packages).

## 5. Book your filmmaker early

Once you know your date and your spot, book the film. We plan with you, shoot on cinema cameras, color grade the film, and set it to licensed music. You get your film in 3 to 4 weeks, depending on the package.

## 6. Enjoy the day

The best part of an elopement is that there is nothing to manage. Show up, say yes, and let us keep it forever.
