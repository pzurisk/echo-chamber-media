Couples ask us the same thing first: what does it cost? Here are our three Las Vegas elopement packages, what you get in each, and how to choose.

## What every package includes

Every package includes planning help, cinema camera coverage, a color graded film, and licensed music. The difference between them is how much time we spend with you and how long the finished film runs.

## The Chapel, $500

Short, sweet, and done right.

- 1 hour of coverage
- Ceremony plus one nearby spot
- A 2 to 3 minute film
- Full ceremony audio
- Delivered in 1 week

Pick this if you want your ceremony captured beautifully and you do not need a long day.

## Downtown, $750

Say your vows, then walk the lights. This is the package behind our Luciano and Muriel film.

- 2 hours of coverage
- Ceremony plus a Fremont Street walk
- A 3 to 4 minute film plus a 30 second teaser
- Full ceremony audio
- Delivered in 1 week

Pick this if you want the ceremony and the neon, with a short teaser you can share right away.

## Desert & Lights, $1,000

Red rock at golden hour, city lights after dark.

- 4 hours of coverage, drive time included
- A desert location plus downtown or the Strip
- A 4 to 5 minute film plus a 30 second teaser
- Drone shots where allowed
- Delivered in 1 week

Pick this if you want the full Las Vegas story, from the landscape to the lights.

## How to choose

Think about how many places you want in your film. One place is The Chapel. A ceremony and a walk is Downtown. A ceremony, the desert, and the city is Desert & Lights.
