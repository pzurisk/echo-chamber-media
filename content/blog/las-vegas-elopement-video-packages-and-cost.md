Our Las Vegas elopement films cost $500 to $1,000. The Chapel is $500 for 1 hour. Downtown is $750 for 2 hours. Desert & Lights is $1,000 for 4 hours. Every film is delivered in 2 weeks.

Here is what you get in each package and how to choose.

## What every package includes

Every package includes planning help, cinema camera coverage, a color graded film, and licensed music. The difference between them is how much time we spend with you, how many places we film, and how long the finished film runs.

## The Chapel, $500

Short, sweet, and done right.

- 1 hour of coverage
- Ceremony plus one nearby spot
- A 2 to 3 minute film
- Full ceremony audio
- Delivered in 2 weeks, ask about rush delivery

Pick this if you want your ceremony captured beautifully and you do not need a long day.

## Downtown, $750

Say your vows, then walk the lights. This is the package behind our Luciano and Muriel film.

- 2 hours of coverage
- Ceremony plus a Fremont Street walk
- A 3 to 4 minute film plus a 30 second teaser
- Full ceremony audio
- Delivered in 2 weeks, ask about rush delivery

Pick this if you want the ceremony and the neon, with a short teaser you can share right away.

## Desert & Lights, $1,000

Red rock at golden hour, city lights after dark.

- 4 hours of coverage, drive time included
- A desert location plus downtown or the Strip
- A 4 to 5 minute film plus a 30 second teaser
- Drone shots where allowed
- Delivered in 2 weeks, ask about rush delivery

Pick this if you want the full Las Vegas story, from the landscape to the lights.

## What changes the price

- **Coverage time.** More hours means more footage to shoot and edit.
- **Number of places.** Each new location adds travel and setup. The Chapel is one spot, Downtown is two, and Desert & Lights is a desert location plus the city.
- **Film length.** A longer film takes longer to cut, color grade, and mix.
- **Delivery speed.** Standard is 2 weeks. Ask about rush delivery if you need it sooner.

## Why an elopement film costs less than a full wedding video

A full wedding video covers a whole day: prep, ceremony, reception, speeches, and dancing. That means long hours and a long edit. An elopement is smaller. Fewer people, one to three places, a shorter timeline, and a shorter film. We break down the bigger picture in our guide to [what a Las Vegas wedding videographer costs](/blog/wedding-videographer-cost-las-vegas).

## How to choose

Think about how many places you want in your film. One place is The Chapel. A ceremony and a walk is Downtown. A ceremony, the desert, and the city is Desert & Lights.
