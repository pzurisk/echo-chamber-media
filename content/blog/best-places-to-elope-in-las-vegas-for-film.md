Where you elope shapes how your film looks and feels. We film in three kinds of places in Las Vegas, and each one gives you a different story.

## A classic chapel

A chapel gives you a real ceremony in a setting made for it. It is the simplest day to plan, because the chapel handles the room and the timing. We coordinate with them on camera rules so the film feels calm and unhurried.

Our Luciano and Muriel film begins at the Little Church of the West. [Watch it on the elopements page](/elopements).

## Downtown and Fremont Street

If you want energy, go downtown. The canopy of gold lights over Fremont Street makes everyone look warm and a little cinematic, even in the middle of the afternoon. It is a great fit if you want to say your vows and then celebrate in the street.

It is also where we shot a lot of Luciano and Muriel's film. You can see the marquee lights, the walk, and the slow dance on the sidewalk at the end.

## The desert

For quiet and scale, go to the desert. Red rock at golden hour looks like a painting, and it gives your film room to breathe. Add a stop downtown or on the Strip afterward and you get both sides of Las Vegas in one film. Where we can fly a drone, we add aerial shots.

## No chapel? No problem

You do not need a chapel to elope. Bring an officiant and your license and we will help you pick a spot. Downtown, the desert, and a rooftop all work.

## How to choose

- Want it simple and quick? A chapel.
- Want lights and energy? Downtown.
- Want space and scenery? The desert, plus the city after dark.
